# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Bot Name]** ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when the Bot is added to a Discord server ("Guild") or used by Discord users ("you").

By using the Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please remove the Bot from any server you manage and refrain from using its commands.

---

## 1. What Data We Collect

The Bot only stores the minimum information needed to run its economy and entertainment features:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Discord User ID** | To identify your virtual wallet, inventory, cooldowns, and giveaway entries |
| **Discord Guild (Server) ID** | To keep each server's economy and settings separate |
| **Role / Channel / Message IDs** | To configure server features (role rewards, giveaway channels, temporary roles) |
| **Virtual currency balances** (cash/bank) | To run the economy, shop, and mini-games |
| **Inventory quantities** | To track items purchased in the server shop |
| **Command cooldown timestamps** | To prevent command spam |
| **Giveaway data** (prize, entries, host ID) | To run and announce giveaways |
| **Server configuration values** set by administrators | To remember your server's custom settings |

### What We Do **NOT** Collect

- ❌ We do **not** read or store the content of your messages.
- ❌ We do **not** collect your email address, phone number, real name, date of birth, or payment information.
- ❌ We do **not** ask for or accept any real-money payments.
- ❌ We do **not** track your activity outside of the commands you run on the Bot itself.

## 2. How We Use the Data

Data is used strictly to operate the Bot's features, such as:

- Storing and displaying your virtual balance and inventory.
- Enforcing command cooldowns.
- Running giveaways and announcing winners.
- Applying server-specific settings configured by server administrators.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Bot's functionality.

## 3. Virtual Currency Disclaimer

All currency, items, and rewards within the Bot are **purely virtual, fictional, and for entertainment purposes only**. They:

- Have **no real-world monetary value**.
- Cannot be purchased with, exchanged for, or withdrawn as real money.
- Have no connection to gambling, betting, or wagering involving real currency.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored in a local SQLite database on the hosting server.
- Access to the hosting server is restricted to the Bot's developer.
- We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party.
- We retain data only for as long as the Bot operates on the relevant server, or until deletion is requested (see Section 6).

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on the Discord platform. Your use of Discord itself is governed by [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). This Privacy Policy covers only the data the Bot itself stores.

## 6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your data at any time.

- **As a user:** Join our [Support Server](SUPPORT_SERVER_LINK) and ask a staff member to delete your data, or contact the developer directly at **[Developer Contact]**.
- **As a server administrator:** You may request removal of **all data** stored for your server, including balances, settings, and inventories.
- **Leaving a server** does not automatically delete your stored data for that server, so a deletion request is recommended if you want it removed.

Deletion requests are typically processed within **72 hours**.

## 7. Children

The Bot is intended for users aged **13 and older**, in line with Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the Bot, contact us and we will delete it.
